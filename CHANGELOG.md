# Changelog

## 1.1.0 (2026-11-17)

- Options now slide out to the right of the window (click the OPTIONS tab) instead of opening below it,
  and the window moves itself back on screen if it would run off the edge.
- The colour graph is now one smooth, glowing curve with a gradient fill, instead of stepped bars.

## 1.0.1 (2026-11-17)

First public release. Fixes garbled symbols (☀, ☾, ·, −) and the crowded preset/wake-time row in 1.0.0.

## 1.0.0 (2026-11-17)

Archived build only, not released.

- Daytime, sunset and bedtime colours, following sunrise and sunset for your location (offline).
- Seven presets, a wake time that sets bedtime, and slow, medium or very fast transitions.
- Turn off for an hour, until sunrise, for full-screen apps, or for chosen apps.
- Hotkeys: Alt+End, Alt+PgUp/PgDn (brightness), Alt+Shift+PgUp/PgDn (colour).
- Movie mode, Darkroom, and Blue sky, Emerald, Salt lamp and Soft white effects.
- "Colour right now" from Ember (1200K) to Daylight (6500K).
- Unlocks Windows' full colour range for very warm colours.
- Portable: settings live in `GraveShift-Data` next to the exe.
